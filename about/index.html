about

I am a biomedical engineer working on fast MRI reconstruction, as a researcher at UMRAM, Bilkent University. I did my PhD at TOBB University of Economics and Technology on diffusion models for cardiac cine MRI, after three years of building medical devices in industry.

I played ice hockey for the Turkish national team; the drills page has animated practice drills. I also build small electronics projects with microcontrollers, and a page about them is on its way.
